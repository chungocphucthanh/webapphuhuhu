# 3.1 Temporal rules R1-R3

The implementation is in [rules.py](rules.py). Each symbol records one calendar day over $\Sigma=\lbrace M,A,N,\texttt{-}\rbrace$: morning, afternoon, night, or rest. The [encoder](encode_strings.py) keeps one symbol per day using `N > A > M` when several classes occur. Define $b(a)=1$ for $a\in W=\lbrace M,A,N\rbrace$ and $b(\texttt{-})=0$.

Each DFA is $\mathcal{A}_ i=(Q_ i,\Sigma,\delta_ i,q_ {0,i},F_ i)$. It accepts $w=w_ 1\cdots w_ n$ exactly when $\widehat{\delta}_ i(q_ {0,i},w)\in F_ i$, where $\widehat{\delta}$ applies the transitions successively. Every `dead` state is rejecting and loops on all symbols. Empty words satisfy all three rules.

## R1: Consecutive working days

$$
L_ 1=\lbrace w\in\Sigma^{\ast}: \forall\quad 3\leq t\leq n,\quad \sum_ {j=t-2}^{t}b(w_ j)\leq 2\rbrace.
$$

Here $Q_ 1=\lbrace \texttt{run0},\texttt{run1},\texttt{run2},\mathrm{dead}\rbrace$, $q_ {0,1}=\texttt{run0}$, and $F_ 1=Q_ 1\setminus\lbrace \mathrm{dead}\rbrace$. The live states `run0`, `run1`, and `run2` record streaks of zero, one, and two working days, respectively.

| State | On M, A, or N | On `-` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `run0` | `run1` | `run0` |
| `run1` | `run2` | `run0` |
| `run2` | `dead` | `run0` |
| `dead` | `dead` | `dead` |

The invariant is that a valid prefix ends in the state for its trailing streak; an invalid prefix ends in `dead`. It holds initially. A rest resets the streak, work extends it, and a third working day enters `dead`. Later symbols cannot remove that violation. Induction on prefix length therefore gives $L(\mathcal{A}_ 1)=L_ 1$.

## R2: Rest after night work

$$
L_ 2=\lbrace w\in\Sigma^{\ast}: \forall\quad 1\leq t<n,\quad w_ t=N\Rightarrow w_ {t+1}=\texttt{-}\rbrace.
$$

Take $Q_ 2=\lbrace \mathrm{free},\mathrm{after\_night},\mathrm{dead}\rbrace$, $q_ {0,2}=\mathrm{free}$, and $F_ 2=\lbrace \mathrm{free},\mathrm{after\_night}\rbrace$.

| State | On M or A | On N | On `-` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `free` | `free` | `after_night` | `free` |
| `after_night` | `dead` | `dead` | `free` |
| `dead` | `dead` | `dead` | `dead` |

For a prefix without a forbidden pair, `after_night` means its last symbol is `N`; `free` means it is empty or ends otherwise. This holds initially. Appending work creates a violation exactly from `after_night`; every other transition preserves the stated memory. A violation remains in `dead`. By induction, acceptance is equivalent to having no `NM`, `NA`, or `NN`, so $L(\mathcal{A}_ 2)=L_ 2$.

## R3: Four working days in seven

$$
c_ t(w)=\sum_ {j=\max(1,t-6)}^{t}b(w_ j),\qquad
L_ 3=\lbrace w\in\Sigma^{\ast}: \forall\quad 1\leq t\leq n,\quad c_ t(w)\leq 4\rbrace.
$$

Let $H=\lbrace h\in\lbrace 0,1\rbrace^6: |h|_ 1\leq 4\rbrace$, where $|h|_ 1$ counts ones. Set $Q_ 3=H\cup\lbrace \mathrm{dead}\rbrace$, $q_ {0,3}=\texttt{w000000}$, and $F_ 3=H$; the state of $h$ is written `w` followed by its six bits. For $h=h_ 1\cdots h_ 6\in H$:

$$
\delta_ 3(h,a)=
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{dead}, & |h|_ 1+b(a)>4,\cr
h_ 2h_ 3h_ 4h_ 5h_ 6b(a), & |h|_ 1+b(a)\leq 4.
\end{cases}
$$

A live state remembers the last six work/rest bits, padded initially with zeros. The invariant holds for the empty prefix. On appending a symbol, $|h|_ 1+b(a)$ is exactly the new window's work count. Exceeding four enters `dead`; otherwise, shifting the history preserves the invariant. Earlier violations remain in `dead`. Induction gives $L(\mathcal{A}_ 3)=L_ 3$.

There are $\sum_ {r=0}^{4}\binom{6}{r}+1=58$ states. Every history is reachable by reading its six bits as rest/work; `MMMMM` reaches the trap. R1 and R2 similarly have 4 and 3 reachable states. These finite constructions prove that all three languages are regular.

## Small examples

| Rule | Accepted word | Rejected word and reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | `MA-N-` | `MAN`: three consecutive working days |
| R2 | `N-M` | `NM`: work immediately follows night work |
| R3 | `MM--MM-` | `MM-MM-M`: five working days in seven, despite satisfying R1 |

## Functions in the code

- `r1()`, `r2()`, and `r3()` return complete DFAs for the corresponding languages.
- `main()` saves [dfa_max2_consecutive.json](dfa_max2_consecutive.json) (R1), [dfa_night_then_rest.json](dfa_night_then_rest.json) (R2), and [dfa_max4_in_7days.json](dfa_max4_in_7days.json) (R3), plus R4 in `extra/`.
- [DFA](dfa.py) validates transitions, evaluates words through `accepts()`, and serializes the five components.

## Limits

R2 checks only adjacent days inside the word, so a terminal `N` is accepted. R3's six initial rest bits define its finite-word boundary and also constrain prefixes shorter than seven days; they provide no evidence about actual work before the horizon. The daily encoding merges same-day assignments, so these rules count working days, not individual sessions or hours.
